A. PURPOSE
I understand that the purpose of this research is to evaluate the usability and usefulness of Orbit, a prototype platform designed to help financial advisors organize clients, appointments, documents, tasks, and follow-ups.

I understand that the research will help the project team identify problems, improve the design, and evaluate how well Orbit supports common advisor workflows.



B. PROCEDURES
I understand that I may be asked to:
- use or review the Orbit prototype;
- complete tasks using fictional or synthetic client information;
- answer an online questionnaire;
- provide feedback about the interface and features;
- answer questions about my experience using the system; and
- participate in an interview or demonstration, if applicable.

I understand that participation will take approximately [X] minutes.
I understand that the study may collect information such as:

- my questionnaire answers;
- my feedback and comments;
- time taken to complete tasks;
- task completion or error information;
- which prototype features I use; and
- basic technical information needed to operate or evaluate the prototype.

I understand that I must not enter real client financial or personal information into the Orbit prototype during this study.

I understand that the project team will use fictional or synthetic client information for testing and evaluation whenever client information is required for a task.

I understand that information collected during the study will be accessed only by authorized members of the research/project team and will be stored using reasonable security measures.

I understand that AI features in the prototype are experimental. AI-generated information may be incorrect, and the prototype is not intended to provide financial, investment, insurance, tax, or legal advice.



C. RISKS AND BENEFITS

Risks
I understand that the risks of participating are expected to be minimal.

I understand that I may experience minor inconvenience, frustration, or discomfort while completing tasks or answering questions about the prototype.

I understand that there is a risk that information provided during the study could be accessed by an unauthorized person. The research team will take reasonable measures to reduce this risk.

I understand that I should not provide real client financial information, identification numbers, health information, account information, passwords, or other sensitive client information during the study.

Benefits
I understand that I may not receive a direct personal benefit from participating.

I understand that my feedback may help improve the Orbit prototype and may contribute to the development of software that can better support financial advisors with administrative and organizational work.
